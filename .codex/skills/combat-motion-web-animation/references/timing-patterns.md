# Timing Patterns

Treat these as starting points, not laws.

## Combat Attack

- windup: `80-180ms`
- travel: `120-220ms`
- hit-stop: `40-90ms`
- impact burst: `120-220ms`
- recovery: `120-240ms`
- total target: `420-850ms`

## Ability Cast

- anticipation: `100-220ms`
- charge: `180-420ms`
- release: `60-140ms`
- payload travel: `120-320ms`
- resolution: `120-260ms`
- total target: `600-1200ms`

## Destroy Resolution

- target callout: `60-140ms`
- fatal hit: `80-180ms`
- vanish or collapse: `120-260ms`
- settle: `80-160ms`
- total target: `340-700ms`

## Card Entry

- spawn: `80-160ms`
- emphasis: `80-180ms`
- settle: `120-200ms`
- total target: `280-500ms`

## Timing Rules

- Keep the strongest contrast at the hit frame.
- Use shorter hit-stop for light hits and longer hit-stop for lethal or heavy hits.
- Do not let residual particles outlast the player's need for the board state.
- If repeated use feels tiring, shorten windup before shortening impact.
